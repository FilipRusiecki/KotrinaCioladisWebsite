### About
ReadyMag copy:

> I am an illustrator based in Carlow, Ireland. I work digitally & traditionally. My current work is focused on collaging and working with traditional mediums to create patterned, layered artworks. My digital work provides variety, I enjoy adding elements created traditionally within the digital illustrations. My personal work consists of exploring themes surrounding motherhood, utilising my own experiences as well as others to create deeply meaningful works while also producing humour into the daily life of parenting. My current passion is providing MAMAzine workshops to postpartum mothers to invite reflection of the first year of motherhood within the zine format. Providing community, creativity and fun within the motherhood experience.

### MAMAzine workshop (ReadyMag + NCAD)

> Zine Workshop for postpartum mothers. Create your own MAMAzine in a zine making workshop catered to mothers. The workshop invites you to reflect on your first year postpartum, documenting your firsts as a mother, within zine format. A creative outlet to an often confusing and overwhelming stage of motherhood. Connect, create and share in a comforting space while experimenting through art forms, of collage, and drawing while learning about zine making.
