Zine Workshop for postpartum mothers

Create your own MAMAzine in a zine making workshop catered to mothers. The workshop invites you to reflect on your first year postpartum, documenting your firsts as a mother, within zine format. A creative outlet to an often confusing and overwhelming stage of motherhood. Connect, create and share in a comforting space while experimenting through art forms, of collage, and drawing while learning about zine making.
